The fork is resolved: Option A wins, with tombstones.

The "delete vs two-way sync" question collapses once you realize delete is the wrong verb for a sync system. The right verbs are tombstone (hide, keep history) and reconcile (detect upstream absence, mark, keep reversible). Under this model there is no second writer, no vector clocks needed, and the resurrect bug becomes trivially solvable with tombstone-reason metadata.

This plan executes that decision: stop the silent-data-loss bleeding → pivot to tombstones → kill the phantom surfaces → wire real resilience → document honestly. Migration to go-cqrs-lite/conflict/v3 is deferred to Phase 6 (nice-to-have, not blocking).
